01 · Wedding website

Alyssa & Liam — with a custom RSVP list

A full wedding site for friends: story, details, gallery, registry, and a custom RSVP flow that keeps the guest list actually usable (not “hope the spreadsheet survives”).

  • RSVP → Google Sheets

    When a guest submits the RSVP, the sheet updates on its own. Nobody has to copy answers over by hand, so the couple just opens the sheet and it’s current.

  • Autofill name

    Guests start typing and their name autofills from the guest list. It cuts down on typos and duplicate entries, and the answers land on the right row.

02 · Coaching

Coaching a U11 girls soccer team

I coach an under-11 girls team. It’s a project like any other: plan the work, run the schedule, work with other people, and change course when something isn’t working.

  • Practice plans

    I write each week’s plan around what the team needs to work on, so every session has a point.

  • Schedules & game time

    I manage the practice and game schedule and the playing time, so the girls get fair minutes and everyone knows where to be.

  • Other coaches

    I stay in contact with the other coaches about fields, times, and changes, so nothing surprises us on game day.

  • Pivoting on the fly

    When a drill flops or the lineup changes at the last minute, I switch to something that works in the moment.
