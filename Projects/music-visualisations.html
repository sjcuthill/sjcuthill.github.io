05 / Data Visualization

Music Visualisations

Listening data, made visible

Turning playlists and patterns into visual form—so music data becomes something you can see, not only stream.

01
The Problem

Why did this project need to exist?

Listening history is rich but opaque. Without a visual layer, patterns in taste, tempo, and time stay buried in lists and APIs.

02
The Approach

How did I approach it?

Start from the data shape, then design visuals that make structure obvious—color, form, and motion as the language of the insight.

03
The Build

What did I actually create?

Visual explorations of music data—full write-up and screenshots coming as I finish packaging this case study.

04
The Result

What came out of it?

A practice space for turning unstructured listening data into clear visual stories.
